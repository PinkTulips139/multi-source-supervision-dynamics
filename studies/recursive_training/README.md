# Recursive training — future work

[Home](../../README.md) · [Roadmap](../../docs/ROADMAP.md)

This page records scope only. No recursive or multi-generation experiment is claimed as completed in the current study.

Future work may investigate how supervision errors propagate through multiple Student generations and whether Source mixing can mitigate specific measured failures. A future study must define generation lineage, retained real supervision, budgets, evaluation and baselines, freeze its protocol and receive explicit training authorization.

The current single-generation findings motivate questions; they do not establish recursive collapse, universal correlation effects or a validated mitigation algorithm. No executable configs or placeholder results are created for this future study.
