The complete 2×2 dataset/Student design was not initially preregistered as one study. Historical protocol status fields describe their freeze-time state, including `NOT_EXECUTED` and authorization false; later execution receipts establish completion. Do not rewrite these historical fields.

An older cross-setting chronology said BANKING77 CONTROL was unavailable. That statement predates the later control adjudications and is superseded for current coverage. The preserved control table records all four settings. Old method-repository scope also predates completed Student experiments; current artifact evidence and current explicit research boundaries govern this package.

Failed-run provenance remains in the original research archive. For example, the BANKING77 XLNet transport interruption was followed by a continuation that reused already validated fits; it was not a new scientific retry or a justification to discard results. Internal transport/debug records are not dumped into public main.

Source identities and paths for the current evidence are in the [manifest](../studies/mlbd2026_wrong_label_organization/manifests/evidence_manifest.json); source hashes identify uncommitted research files independently of Git HEAD.
